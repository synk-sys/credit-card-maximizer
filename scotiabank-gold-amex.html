The best move. Book travel anywhere you like (an airline website, Airbnb, a cruise line), pay with this card, then use Apply Points to Travel on sceneplus.ca within 12 months. You get full 1¢ value with no blackout dates, and you keep the airline's own fare.

Avoid account-credit redemptions (0.67¢). Use groceries or travel instead. At 1¢ a point, 6× is effectively 6% back at Sobeys-family stores and 5× is 5% on dining.

4. No foreign transaction fees

What it saves

Most Canadian cards add 2.5% to every foreign-currency purchase. This one charges only the exchange rate. On $5,000 of travel or US online shopping, that saves $125.

The catch

Foreign-currency purchases earn only 1× (even restaurants), so you get about 1% back plus the 2.5% saving. It's still among the best Canadian cards to use abroad.

Acceptance: Amex isn't taken everywhere abroad, especially at small merchants in Europe and Asia. Bring a no-FX Visa or Mastercard as backup. In Canada, Costco takes only Mastercard.
