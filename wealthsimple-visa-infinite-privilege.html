WhatRateFine print
All eligible purchases, including in foreign currency2%No categories, no annual cap. Foreign purchases convert at the Visa network rate with no added FX fee.

Redeeming is simple. Move your cash back to your Wealthsimple chequing account (it earns up to 2.25% interest there), or to a TFSA, RRSP or non-registered account, at any time. There's no minimum. Unredeemed cash back is lost if you close the card, so sweep it regularly.

Bill payments: paying bills through Wealthsimple's Bill Pay takes money from chequing, not the card, so it earns nothing. To earn 2%, give the biller your card number directly.

3. Eligibility and the fee waiver

To qualify

An active Wealthsimple chequing account, plus one of: $150,000 personal income, $200,000 household income, $50,000 a year of card spending, or $400,000 in assets with Wealthsimple. Pre-approval doesn't affect your credit score.

To get the $240 fee waived

$100,000+ in individual Wealthsimple assets (Premium or Generation status), or $4,000+ a month of direct deposits (a paycheque) into Wealthsimple chequing. Otherwise it's $20 a month (billed yearly in Quebec).

Do the maths before moving money. Waiving the fee is worth $240 a year. Moving $100,000 of investments is only worth it if you'd be happy with Wealthsimple's products anyway. Switching a paycheque is the easier route for most people. The metal card is for clients with $100,000+ in assets.

4. Travel perks

Airport lounges

6 lounge passes through the Visa Airport Companion Program (DragonPass network, 1,200+ lounges). Enrol in the Visa Airport Companion app using this card. Wealthsimple doesn't publish guest rules or the price of extra visits, so check the app after you enrol.

No FX fee

Most cards add 2.5% on foreign purchases. This one doesn't, and you still earn the full 2%, so you're about 4.5% ahead of a typical card abroad.

Airport extras

Priority security, parking and VIP offers at select airports. Which airports isn't published, so check before you travel.

Visa Infinite Privilege perks

Luxury Hotel Collection, the Dining Series, Wine Country program, priority reservations at top restaurants, and a 24/7 concierge.
