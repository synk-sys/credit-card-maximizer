2. Amex Offers: the ongoing money

Offers are the part most holders ignore and the part that decides whether the card beats its fee. They are targeted, so you may not see every one below.

The routine

  1. Open the Amex app → Offers once a week.
  2. Add to Card before you pay. Spending first never counts.
  3. Use the Platinum itself, online or in-store as the offer states.
  4. Check the minimum, the cap and the expiry, then calendar it.
  5. Credits typically post in 5–10 business days, longer for some partners.

Stacking that works

  • Amex Offer + a retailer's own sale or portal cash back.
  • Instacart and Walmart+ credits are separate from any retailer offer you see.
  • Marriott/Best Western/Turo-style offers beat the Platinum's base earn.
  • Offers are per card. Supplementary holders may see different ones.

3. Lounge access: what changes 1 Jan 2027

Plaza Premium and Priority Pass visits become capped at 6 each per calendar year, and guests count against the cap, unless you spend $20,000 in eligible purchases. Centurion, Delta Sky Club (on Delta) and other partner lounges are not affected by this cap. Lufthansa lounges are removed from 1 Oct 2026.

Eligible spend in a calendar year$20,000 removes the cap, for that year and the next

Supplementary cards: supplementary Platinum cards get 2 visits each, supplementary Gold gets 0. Confirm this against your Amex notice before planning a trip around it.

Fine Hotels + Resorts bookings through Amex Travel are where the real hotel value is: breakfast for two, noon check-in, 4pm checkout and a property credit.

5. Toronto Pearson (YYZ)

Priority Security Lane

Terminals 1 and 3. Get the QR pass through the Amex link (go.amex/yyz) and show it at the dedicated lane. Save it to your phone wallet before you leave home.

Valet and parking

Terminal 1 valet is a Platinum perk. Express and Daily Park get 15% back as a statement credit.

Restaurants

The $30-on-$60 Pearson offer runs to 31 Dec 2026. Register before you order.

Lounge options

Plaza Premium and Priority Pass count against the 2027 cap. Check which lounges you are actually choosing.
