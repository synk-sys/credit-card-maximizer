2. How you earn, before and after 18 Nov

Two things decide your rate: whether you hold an eligible Rogers, Fido, Shaw or Comwave service (Rogers calls this being an "Eligible 2% Customer"), and whether you have passed the $61,000 annual spend cap.

* Before the change, Rogers bills earned your normal rate, but cash back redeemed against them was worth 1.5×. That made Rogers bills an effective 3% (2% × 1.5), and it also made all your other cash back worth 1.5× if you spent it on Rogers bills.

Who loses. If your Rogers bills were big enough to soak up your whole cash back balance at 1.5×, every purchase was effectively earning 3% (2% × 1.5). After 18 Nov, only Rogers purchases earn the higher rate, and everything else drops to a plain 2%. Heavy spenders with small phone bills lose the most. Run your numbers in the calculator.

The cap: the $61,000 counts all eligible purchases and resets on your account-opening anniversary (or your product-switch anniversary). Once you pass it, everything earns 1.5%, including Rogers bills and USD purchases.

3. What earns 5%

Rogers Bank's definition from 18 Nov 2026. Rogers can change this list at any time without notice, so check "Earned Rewards History" in the app to confirm a purchase was tagged.

Counts

  • Bill payments to Rogers, Fido, Shaw or Comwave, made online or by autopay
  • Online purchases from rogers.com or fido.ca, such as phones and accessories
  • Purchases from TSC (Today's Shopping Choice)
  • Direct subscriptions to Sportsnet+, CityTV+ or Discovery+
  • Jays Shop purchases at Rogers Centre

Does not count

  • Anything bought in a Rogers, Fido, Shaw or Comwave store, including bill payments made in-store
  • Toronto Blue Jays tickets
  • Discounted plans through the Rogers Preferred Program (employer or association pricing)
  • Subscriptions bought through a third party, such as an app store or a TV provider
Biggest easy win. Put every Rogers, Fido, Shaw and Comwave bill in your household on autopay with this card. A $150/month bundle earns $90 a year at 5%, compared with $36 at 2%. Buy your next phone online instead of in-store: on a $1,400 phone, that's $70 instead of $28.

4. US dollars and foreign currency

Every foreign-currency purchase carries a 2.5% conversion fee on top of Mastercard's exchange rate. The earn rate decides whether that fee is worth paying.

Use another card outside the US. In Europe, Mexico or Asia this card costs you money on every purchase. A no-FX-fee card is the better choice there. In the US, the 0.5% net is small but positive. After the $61,000 cap, USD purchases earn only 1.5%, so they cost you 1% net.

5. Roaming: days become a credit

Until 11 Jan 2027

5 Roam Like Home days a year (US or international) for a linked postpaid Rogers mobile account. Fido, Shaw, chatr and business plans don't qualify. Rogers' daily rate is $16/day in the US and $18/day internationally, so 5 days is worth $80–$90. Any phone on the linked account can use them, first come, first served.

From 12 Jan 2027

A $75 annual roaming credit instead. It covers Rogers Travel Passes or Roam Like Home daily charges that you pay with this card. It is not automatic: you submit a reimbursement request in the Rogers Bank app or online banking within 90 days of paying, and the credit can take several statement cycles to post.

Use your days. Any Roam Like Home days left on 12 Jan 2027 are deleted. If you have a December or early-January trip, use them. Unused credit doesn't carry over either: it resets on your account anniversary.

Not covered: a monthly plan that already includes roaming is not an "eligible roaming service", so the credit only pays for add-on roaming.

7. Redeeming cash back

The rules

  • Minimum redemption: $10.
  • Redeem against a purchase posted in the last 90 days, in the Rogers Bank app or online banking.
  • You can only redeem against purchases posted after your balance reached $10.
  • From 18 Nov: 1 point = $0.01 on everything, Rogers bills included.
  • Rewards can take several months to appear in your rewards balance.

Annual statement credit

Prefer a lump sum? Call 1-855-775-2265 by 1 December and your full balance is credited in January. Redemptions never count toward your minimum payment, so keep paying the statement.

Deadline: 17 Nov 2026. The 1.5× bonus ends on 18 Nov, even for Rogers charges that posted earlier. If you have $40 of cash back and a Rogers bill posted in the last 90 days, redeem it now in the app: it's worth $60 today and $40 after the change.
