3. Air Canada perks

Free first checked bag

For the primary cardholder, additional cardholders, and up to 8 companions on the same reservation, up to 23 kg. Applies when the trip starts on an Air Canada flight (including Rouge and Air Canada Express), whether you paid cash or points.

Faster Elite status

1,000 Status Qualifying Credits (SQC) for every $20,000 of spending, up to 25,000 SQC a year across all your Aeroplan credit cards. SQC count toward status only; you can't redeem them.

NEXUS rebate

Up to $100 back on the NEXUS application fee once every 48 months, for the primary cardholder and additional cardholders.

1.5× on Air Canada

Flights and Air Canada Vacations bought directly from Air Canada earn the bonus rate, on top of what you earn from the flight itself.

What the bag is worth: Air Canada charges for the first checked bag on most economy fares. A family of four checking one bag each on a return trip can save well over $200 on a single trip. Additional cardholders must link their Aeroplan numbers to get the benefit.

4. Redeeming Aeroplan points, after the June 2026 changes

TD values Aeroplan points at 2¢ or more for most flight redemptions. You get that by booking flights, not by spending points on merchandise or gift cards.

Practical rules. Air Canada flights use dynamic pricing, so check the points price against the cash fare before you book. Partner awards (Star Alliance) use the fixed chart, and partner business class is where you get 2¢+ a point. Avoid merchandise and gift cards, which are usually worth under 1¢.

Source note: the chart changes are summarized from Milesopedia and Upgraded Points. Check the current chart on aircanada.com before you plan a big redemption.
