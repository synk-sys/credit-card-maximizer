The weak spot. One point per dollar is low for a $120 card. The card makes sense only if you redeem through the flight chart below at close to 2¢ a point. If you redeem at 1¢ (100 points = $1), a no-fee cash back card will usually beat it.

Points don't expire while your account is open. They're cancelled 12 months after you close the card, so redeem or transfer them before you cancel.

3. The fixed-points flight chart

Avion's Air Travel Redemption Schedule. Every round-trip economy flight in a zone costs the same number of points, up to a maximum ticket price (before taxes and fees). Any airline, any date, no blackouts. If the ticket costs more than the maximum, you pay the difference with your card or with more points.

How to win with the chart. Book when the base fare (before taxes) is close to the maximum. A $700 Toronto–Vancouver fare for 35,000 points is worth 2¢ a point. A $280 fare in the same zone is only 0.8¢, so pay that one with points at 100 = $1, or with cash. Short regional hops at peak times are often the best value of all.

Business class doesn't use the chart: it costs 100 points per $1. Taxes and fees are always extra, but you can pay them with points at 100 = $1.

5. Linked perks most people forget

Petro-Canada

Link your Petro-Points card: 3¢/L off at the pump, 20% more Petro-Points and 20% more Avion points. Linking can take 2 business days.

DoorDash DashPass

12 months free: $0 delivery fees on orders of $15+ from eligible restaurants. It auto-renews at full price afterwards, so set a reminder to cancel.

Rexall Be Well

Link your card and earn 50 Be Well points per $1 at Rexall, on top of your Avion points.

Canadian Tire Triangle

3× Canadian Tire Money at Canadian Tire, Sport Chek, Mark's and more when you scan your linked Triangle card.

Visa Infinite perks

Visa Infinite Luxury Hotel Collection (900+ hotels with upgrades and breakfast), the Dining Series, wine-country events, and discounts at Park'N Fly, Via Rail and Viator.

GigSky data and Concierge

1 GB of free eSIM data for 15 days in 175+ countries, plus 20% off GigSky plans. Free 24/7 Visa Infinite Concierge: 1-888-853-4458.

Not included: no airport lounge access, and no fast-track security. Those come with the $399 Avion Visa Infinite Privilege.
