American Express Cobalt® Card Maximizer

The best grocery and restaurant card in Canada, for now. It earns 5 points per dollar on up to $2,500 of food spending a month, but it costs $15.99 a month, and its fixed-points flight chart disappears on 6 January 2027. Here's what to do before then, how to stay inside the 5× rules, and a calculator to check whether the card pays for itself.

It's the merchant code that counts, not what you buy. Amex pays 5× based on how the store is coded. Stand-alone grocers such as Metro, Sobeys, IGA, Safeway and Whole Foods usually earn 5×. Big-box and superstore chains, pharmacies and warehouse clubs often earn only 1×, and convenience stores (7-Eleven, Couche-Tard) are excluded. After your first shop at a new store, check your statement to see how it coded.

The cap in practice: $2,500 a month at 5× is 12,500 points, or 150,000 points a year. Above the cap, food earns 1×, so a second card may be better for that spending.

3. Fixed Points Travel: gone on 6 Jan 2027

Amex confirmed in October 2026 that the Cobalt (along with the Gold Rewards, Green and Business Gold) loses Fixed Points Travel from 6 January 2027. Platinum and Business Platinum keep it, but only by phone. A fixed number of points covers the base fare of a return economy flight, up to a maximum.

Before 6 January. If a flight you're planning prices near the zone maximum, book it with Fixed Points now. Unused value below the maximum is lost: it isn't applied to taxes and isn't refunded. After the cutoff, your points can still be used for travel at 1¢ each (1,000 points = $10), or transferred to Aeroplan and the other partners below, which is usually the better option anyway.

5. Perks worth using

Instacart credit

Up to $60 in statement credits per 12 months on Instacart orders in Canada, until 31 Dec 2027. Credits are earned monthly, so check the conditions when you register.

The Hotel Collection

Book 2+ consecutive nights through Amex Travel: up to US$100 hotel credit, noon check-in, 4pm late checkout and an upgrade when available. Compare the price with booking direct first.

Amex Experiences

Front Of The Line presales and reserved tickets. A Cardmember entrance and the American Express Lounge at Rogers Stadium and RBC Amphitheatre.

Amex Offers and Member extras

Targeted statement credits and bonus points. Add them in the app before you shop.

Refer a friend

Earn bonus points for each approved referral, up to 225,000 points per calendar year.

Free additional cards

No fee for additional cards. A partner's grocery and restaurant spending earns 5× too, within the same $2,500 monthly cap.

Not included: no lounge access, no travel credit, no NEXUS credit, and no trip cancellation or interruption insurance.

Abroad: you earn 1 point and pay a 2.5% fee, so the card loses money on every foreign purchase. Keep it for groceries and restaurants in Canada.

Acceptance: Amex isn't accepted everywhere: Costco in Canada, for example, takes only Mastercard. Keep a Visa or Mastercard as a backup.
